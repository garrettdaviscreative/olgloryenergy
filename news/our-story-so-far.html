Our Story So Far, and Where We’re Headed

By the Ol’ Glory crew

Every can of Ol’ Glory starts with one man’s idea: energy drinks could use a little more razzle dazzle. This is the story of where that idea came from, and where we’re taking it next.

A showman from Ripley

Ol’ Glory was created by Don Sessions. Don was a Ripley native who spent years in Los Angeles before setting up shop in a warehouse near downtown Oklahoma City. He had a showman’s instincts and a patriot’s heart, and he didn’t see any reason the two shouldn’t share a label.

Before the energy drink, the Ol’ Glory name went on a whole lineup of drinks for adults. Don’s Ol’ Glory Beverages included Victory Vodka, America’s Whiskey, R&R wines, Grandpa’s Moonshine, a spiced rum and a tequila. Subtle? No. Memorable? Absolutely. That was the point.

The fight for the flag

In 2010, Don wanted to put the American flag and the Pledge of Allegiance on the label of his Ol’ Glory beer. Federal regulators said no.

Most people would have redesigned the label and moved on. Don was 75 years old at the time, and he wasn’t most people. He argued that putting the flag on his own product was his First Amendment right, took the fight public and ended up on national news.

In the end, the label was approved with a government disclaimer added, and the flag stayed right where Don put it. That fight says a lot about the brand you’re drinking today. The flag on an Ol’ Glory can isn’t decoration. Somebody stood up for it.

America’s Energy Drink

The same spirit went into Ol’ Glory Energy. The early cans didn’t exactly whisper. The original Citrus Sugar Free can was covered in red and white stripes, with an eagle-headed script logo and the full Pledge of Allegiance printed right on the side.

The idea was simple: a fizzy, firework-like energy drink that makes you want to put your hand over your heart. It caught on. Ol’ Glory is now sold in 42 states. (The other 8 are on notice.)

Remembering Don

Don Sessions passed away in 2019. He didn’t get to see everything Ol’ Glory has become, but none of it would exist without him. His fight for the flag, his love of a good show and his refusal to take no for an answer are baked into every can. We carry that forward with purpose.

What’s in the can today

Today’s lineup is three flavors, all in 16 fl oz cans with 153mg of caffeine:

  • Citrus, the original. Bright, sharp and loud enough to wake the neighbors.
  • Citrus Zero. Zero sugar, zero apologies, same kick.
  • Mixed Berry. Strawberry, raspberry and blackberry, reporting for duty.

You’ll find them at gas stations, convenience stores and grocers across 42 states, usually for $1.99 a can.

Where we’re headed

Here’s the honest version: a brand with this much history deserves to look and sound as bold as it tastes. So we’re giving Ol’ Glory a refresh. Same name, same flag, same attitude, with a sharper look and a louder voice.

You’re looking at part of it right now. The new olglory.com is our home base, with everything from caffeine content to where the flag story started. Behind the scenes, there’s more design work underway, and we’ll show it off as it’s ready.

We’re also working on getting Ol’ Glory into more coolers. Forty-two states is a great start, but we didn’t name a drink after the flag to stop short of all fifty. If your local gas station or grocer doesn’t carry Ol’ Glory yet, ask them to stock it. Every request helps.

Why we started this page

This News section is where we’ll post weekly updates. Expect flavor news, behind-the-scenes looks at the refresh, where you can find us, stories from the people who drink Ol’ Glory and, now and then, a little more history.

Don built this brand on the idea that you shouldn’t be quiet about the things you’re proud of. We don’t plan to start now.

See you next week, patriot.
