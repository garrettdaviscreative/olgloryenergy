Straight answers

Questions? Canswers.

How much caffeine is in Ol’ Glory?

Every 16 fl oz can has 153mg of caffeine, in all three flavors. Not recommended for children, pregnant or nursing women, or people sensitive to caffeine.

What flavors does Ol’ Glory come in?

Three: Citrus, the original; Citrus Zero, with zero sugar; and Mixed Berry.

Is there a sugar-free Ol’ Glory?

Yes. Citrus Zero has 0g of sugar and the same 153mg of caffeine as the original.

How much sugar is in Citrus and Mixed Berry?

Each has 53g of total sugars and 221 calories per 16 fl oz can. Want the kick without the sugar? Grab Citrus Zero.

Where can I buy Ol’ Glory?

At gas stations, convenience stores and grocers in 42 states, usually for $1.99 a can. Don’t see it at your local spot? Ask them to stock it.

Can I buy Ol’ Glory online?

Not right now. For now, you’ll find Ol’ Glory in stores across 42 states.

Who founded Ol’ Glory?

Ol’ Glory was created by Don Sessions, who wanted to put some razzle dazzle behind energy drinks.

Why is it called Ol’ Glory?

The name comes from Old Glory, the classic nickname for the American flag. That’s why the flag has always been on the can.
