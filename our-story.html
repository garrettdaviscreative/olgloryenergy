Our story

The man behind the razzle dazzle.

Nothing in America like it.

There’s nothing in America like Ol’ Glory. Sipping on a fizzy, firework-like, refreshing Ol’ Glory will have you placing your hand over your heart faster than you can say “Red, white and blue.” This is exactly what Don Sessions envisioned when he created our glorious energy drink, which is now sold in 42 states! ’Murica!

A Ripley native who spent years in Los Angeles, Don set up shop in a warehouse near downtown Oklahoma City with one idea: energy drinks deserved a little more showmanship.

2010 · The fight for the flag

They said no flag. Don didn’t fold.

In 2010, federal regulators turned down the label for Don’s Ol’ Glory beer because it carried the American flag and the Pledge of Allegiance. Don was 75 years old, and he wasn’t about to back down. He argued it was his First Amendment right, took the fight public and landed on national news.

In the end, the label was approved with a government disclaimer added, and the flag stayed right where Don put it: on the can.
